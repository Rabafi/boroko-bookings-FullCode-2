# Artwork prompts

## Cover: bar-handbook-cover.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: A4 portrait cover artwork for a premium hospitality operations handbook.
Prompt: Premium editorial hospitality photograph for a bar operations handbook. An orderly contemporary Southern African lounge counter, warm natural wood, subtle brass details, clean glassware, soft morning preparation light, an unbranded point-of-sale device with its screen turned away. Calm, professional and welcoming. No readable text, no logos, no customer information. Portrait composition with generous quiet space in the upper third for separately typeset title. Restrained charcoal, cream, warm gold and muted sage palette, realistic materials, no clutter.
Avoid: readable words, labels, logos, screens facing the viewer, people, brand marks, watermarks, clutter, neon colors, fake UI, fake receipt, distorted glassware.

## Section still life: bar-service-still-life.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: small landscape section illustration for a premium bar operations handbook.
Prompt: Editorial still-life of a prepared bar service station: neatly arranged glassware, a clean tray, a small stack of blank inventory count cards, brass tray, warm wood, muted sage cloth, soft morning light. No screens, no logos, no readable text, no people, no customer information. Calm negative space for a chapter caption. Realistic hospitality editorial photography with charcoal, cream, warm gold and muted sage palette.
Avoid: readable words, logos, brand marks, fake application screens, fake receipt, clutter, neon, hands, faces, watermarks.
