# Tsa Bonno Bar Customer Manual

Documented version: **1.5.7**  
Review date: **10 September 2026**  
Operating mode: **Bar**  
Example outlet: **Main Bar**  
Example currency: **P**

This editable manuscript is the customer-facing content source for the ReportLab layout in `build/build_manual.py`. It is intended for owners, managers, cashiers, bartenders, stock controllers and finance users.

## Using this guide

Each procedure states its purpose, what is needed first, where to go, the numbered actions, the result to check, and the safest recovery action. A shared Till still requires each operator to use a private PIN. A missing option usually means the role, outlet or package does not allow it; ask a manager or Tsa Bonno support to check access.

Money safety rule: **Preserve the original attempt. Open Manage > Sales report or My sales to check the original receipt status, and use Retry original in Sell only when the original-attempt banner shows it. Confirm the result before initiating another transaction.**

## Find what you need

- Owner: setup acceptance, cash-up, reports, packages, backup and support.
- Manager: staff, permissions, approvals, corrections, stock, cash-up and close blockers.
- Cashier or bartender: shift opening, Till unlock, selling, payment, tabs, receipts and handover.
- Stock controller: products, deliveries, counts, breakage, availability and movement history.
- Finance user: cash-up review, expenses, supplier bills, ledger, reconciliation, payroll and statements.

## Part A — Welcome

The Bar workspace rail exposes Sell, Open tabs and Products. Stock counts, Cash & close and the Sales report live under Manage: choose Manage at the bottom of the sidebar and enter your manager PIN (managers and admins only; unlocked for 15 minutes on that device; works offline after the team list has synced once on that computer). Search results that open a Manage page ask for the same manager PIN. Essential terms are Till, outlet, shift, basket, tab, tender, pending and unavailable. The first-sale path is: confirm setup, open the shift, unlock the Till when asked, build the basket, choose the tender, confirm the completed sale and keep its receipt reference.

## Part B — Install and configure

Use a verified Tsa Bonno installer and a supported Windows computer. Sign in with the business account supplied to the customer, choose the approved business and outlet, confirm Bar mode, review currency/date/receipt details in Manage > Settings, create products and packs, set opening stock, create staff in Manage > Staff accounts, and open Manage > System Health > Devices & displays to work the numbered setup steps in order: receipt printer (pick a detected Windows printer, find network printers, or enter the direct-print target), cash drawer (tick Manual drawer for a drawer with no printer — it opens with the key), barcode scanner (one real scan), pinned screens, and card machine (Manual works with any bank machine; bridge mode needs the bank's till-integration address) — then Save and use Test all devices. Pin the customer display to the guest-facing monitor so it reopens there after restart, and optionally tick Open customer display on startup.

The setup acceptance check is complete when a demonstration operator can open a shift, make a demonstration sale, find it in Manage > Sales report, print a readable receipt, find an open tab and explain the closing path.

## Part C — Run a shift

Open the shift by typing the Opening cash float explicitly — type 0.00 when starting with no cash — and choose Start my shift. An empty float never starts a shift. If the Till asks, choose Unlock Till, select the operator in Who is taking this order?, enter Staff PIN and choose Unlock Till. A wrong Staff PIN says to ask an admin to reset it in Staff Management. Each operator sets their own PIN in My Shift > My Staff PIN while signed in (profile menu > My Staff PIN; admins keep the Staff Management override); if the current PIN is forgotten, ask an admin to reset it. On Sell, build and correct the unpaid basket before payment. My Shift shows a handover for the open shift: completed sales count, open tabs, waste since the shift started, and cash-up state — counts and names only, never takings.

On a shared terminal, one screen closes the whole shift: open Staff shift close, choose the name once and enter one Staff PIN. Clock in, and the first person on shift is taken straight to the Shared drawer tab. A shared drawer opens once per outlet per day: count the starting float, enter who counted it, and open. Sellers then clock in and out freely with no personal cash-up — all cash goes in the one drawer. Record drops to the safe and paid-outs (paid-outs need a reason) as they happen, each proven by the operator's own Staff PIN entered on the drawer card itself, and record a handover count whenever sellers change hands (handovers move no money). The Till shows Drawer not open and refuses sales while no drawer period is open. People who never touched the Till clock out without a cash-up from the Shift tab.

Pin fast sellers with the star on a product card; pinned items gather under the ★ Favourites filter and measured best sellers gather under Top sellers. Change a basket line with −, +, or by typing the quantity directly. A removed line can be restored with Undo. Clearing the whole basket asks for confirmation first. Touching the basket again after a failed payment clears the old error so the next Pay is judged fresh. An empty basket offers Same again to repeat the last sale (unavailable lines are skipped and named) and, where pool tables exist, Pool night to add every table at once before typing each box cash. Finished products grey out by themselves with Finished — out of stock and refuse extra taps (Only 2 Heineken left); the Till re-checks counts quietly after every sale, and the server still refuses any over-sale at Pay. Low badges such as Only 2 bottles left appear on cards running low; they are guidance only and never block a sale.

For Cash, enter the cash received before Pay works — tap Exact, P20, P50, P100 or P200, or type the amount received. Pay stays blocked while the box is empty so every cash receipt carries received and change. The Till shows the change due, prints it on the receipt, and leaves it big on the guest screen so the customer sees it without crowding the till; the receipt also says to close the cash drawer. The sale itself always records exactly the amount due, and the extra is change, never extra revenue. Card and Mobile money are collected through the approved external provider. Manual card mode works with any machine: charge the card on the machine as usual, then enter the machine approval code at the Till (optional). Where the supplier has fitted a bridge, a manager can enter the provider and bridge address in System Health > Devices & displays and the Till offers Send to card machine, which fills the approval code automatically — the sale itself is still confirmed with Pay and never charges twice. A missing printed receipt is not a reason to collect again: use the completed-sale POS Receipt overlay and choose Print receipt when it is open, or choose Reprint last receipt from an empty Sell basket to reprint this terminal's most recent receipt without charging again.

## Part D — Tabs and corrections

Create and name a tab from Sell, reopen it through Open tabs > Resume tab → to keep selling, or choose Settle to reopen it with payment already open. With two tills on one network, stock counts include the other till's unsent sales (the Till says so and plays it safe until they send), and a tab being settled on the other till shows Settling on the other till — wait a moment instead of paying twice. The Till's Open tabs count always matches the Open tabs page (same server read, refreshed while you sell). Resume and Settle are different actions: resuming never charges. Confirm the owner and outlet; a resumed tab shows Ready to continue, or Tab changed — refresh required when it must be reopened from Open tabs. Split uses Split, Number of checks and Split checks. Transfer uses Transfer waiter, Active waiter for this outlet, Choose waiter and Transfer waiter. Final settlement uses Record payment & close tab.

If a split is uncertain, use Open tabs > Unresolved operations > Check status, then use Retry original only when that split record says it is safe. If a transfer is uncertain, check its transfer record in the same view. Settlement uncertainty is separate: check Manage > Sales report or My sales for the original settlement and use Retry original in Sell only when the original-attempt banner shows it. Do not repeat an action from memory.

An unpaid basket edit, a recorded-sale correction/void, a stock return and a money refund are different events with different records and approvals. The operator requests a correction from My shift > Request sale correction, completes Reason and detail, and asks an authorised approver to enter the Authorised approver PIN and choose Ask approver to confirm. The request does not itself return money.

## Part E — Products and stock

Add a product and its stock in one flow from Products > Add product or Manage > Stock counts > Add stock item: name, shared category, selling price and optional barcode first then the counted stock behind it (create matching stock, or link existing stock), the counted unit, outlet, opening quantity and low-stock threshold, then optional 6/12/24 packs with their own barcodes. A new product sells at the Till only after its catalog publication completes: Products keeps pending saves with Retry this save, and a Till sale blocked by a stale menu says to refresh explicitly — nothing is charged for the blocked attempt. The review line states the depletion plainly, for example one sale removes one bottle and a 6-pack removes six bottles. Saving twice or after an interruption never duplicates the product: an interrupted save can be retried from Products, where waiting saves show Retry this save. A product or stock name can only be used once: if a save is refused because the name already exists, use the existing product instead of saving a second one. Deleting a product removes it from the Till; a product with sale history is archived instead so reports stay intact. When nothing else sells or uses its stock, the stock item is delisted from Stock automatically while its movement history is preserved for audit, and the freed name can be used again. Stock linked only to archived products is hidden in Stock by default to reduce clutter — tick Show archived to audit, count or receive it; its history stays available. Editing reuses the same flow but never replays opening stock; use Receive or Count for quantity changes. Items counted but not sold directly use the Stock only choice. Recipe-linked items show their stock setup as read-only and keep their ingredients management in Stock & Purchasing Pro.

Use Manage > Stock counts > Receive delivery for stock that physically arrived, or choose Receive on a product card to jump straight to receiving its linked stock. Products names finished items itself: counted products at zero carry Out of stock, low ones carry Only X left with the on-hand count on their stock line, and the header counts them. Use Count for one item or Count All for a full list. Type 0 when the physical quantity is zero; an empty field is not zero. Record breakage or wastage as the supported stock event. Mark a product unavailable only when it should not be sold. Use the Waste action on a stock row for burnt or spoilt food, breakage, spillage or expired items: enter the wasted quantity with a reason and note, and the removal is recorded as one auditable movement.

Simple food can consume weighed ingredients without the recipes package: count Potatoes in kilograms with an opening quantity such as 10, then set the Fries product to consume 0.3 stock units per sale so each sale removes 0.3 kg. Sizes and extras (Small/Large, fillings) are modifier groups: the product wizard shows which groups cover the chosen category, and Till offers them at sale time instead of needing duplicate products. A 6-pack works for portions as well as bottles: a 6-pack of fatcakes removes 6 portions. Pack sizes stay 6, 12 and 24.

Food cooked in-house by the tray, such as hundreds of fatcakes a day, can skip stock entirely: choose No stock tracking when adding the product. Sales record revenue only and deplete nothing, so mark the product unavailable yourself when the tray runs out. This choice is not available for recipe-forced sections.

Each pool table is one product in the Pool category: name it Pool Table 1, Pool Table 2, choose Pool, keep price P1 and choose No stock tracking with no packs. Price must stay P1 so the quantity typed at the Till equals pula collected. Add one product per physical table; when a table is broken mark that table product unavailable. A table created earlier with stock attached keeps selling at the Till, but delete it in Products and re-add it as Pool with No stock tracking when convenient so its old stock does not linger.

Worked example: 48 opening bottles + 24 received − 19 sold − 2 broken = 51 expected. A physical count of 50 gives a variance of −1 bottle.

## Part F — Cash-up and reports

Cash & close is part of the base Bar workflow; the Accounting add-on is not required for Bar cash-up. The operator opens My Cash-up, enters Physical cash counted (P), and chooses Submit cash-up for review. A manager opens Manage with the manager PIN, then Cash & close, chooses Refresh, reviews Expected cash, Counted cash, Variance and Cash-up evidence, then chooses Return for correction or enters the Manager PIN and chooses Approve & close shift. Unavailable evidence is not zero activity.

One shared drawer closes once per outlet per day: Cash & close > Drawer close, count the drawer blind (no expected total is ever shown, not even to managers) and submit. A manager reviews it against the server total on the same page and approves or returns it with a correction note; approval closes the period and quiets empty Till shifts. Cash & close also holds the outlet cash question: How this outlet counts cash switches between One shared drawer and Separate pouches (admin only, and only with zero open Till shifts, drawer periods and reviews).

Manage > Sales report supports From and To dates, receipt/tab/operator/tender search, completed and exception review, receipt detail and supported exports. My sales always shows your saved figures; until the server confirms them they carry a * estimate mark (last saved on this device, not confirmed totals), and corrections still need confirmed figures. The Sales report also carries a Waste card: quantities wasted per item with the top reason for the selected period, taken from the Waste actions only. A Slow movers card flags stocked items with no sale in the last 14 days, quantities only. A Pool tables card shows each table separately with its collected cash for the period plus an All pool tables total: at night add each table product to one counter sale, type the cash from each table box as its quantity (Table 1 P80 = quantity 80), then Pay cash once. Pool tables pay Cash only: card, mobile money, split and account charge are disabled for a sale holding Pool lines, and the basket keeps one compact row per item with a small quantity box so several items fit without scrolling. Costed waste stays in Stock & Purchasing Pro. The Excel export adds Waste Summary and Waste Detail sheets and the PDF adds a waste section with the same quantities; the JSON companion carries the same waste dataset. Cashier cash-up and Accounting close are separate records.

## Part G — Optional packages

Stock & Purchasing Pro adds supplier, purchasing, detailed receiving, lots/expiry, recipes, preparation and variance work. Accounting & Workforce adds expenses, workforce, payroll, tips, supplier bills, ledger, bank reconciliation and financial reporting. Growth & Multi-outlet adds customers, loyalty, vouchers, promotions, multiple outlets and advanced reporting.

Daily workflows include Manage > Bar expenses > Record expense, Manage > Supplier bills, Manage > Accounting, General ledger, Bank reconciliation, Financial statements, Payroll, Workforce, Customers & loyalty, Vouchers, Multi-outlet control and Growth analytics. Package access, staff permission, outlet scope and the visible task screen are separate requirements.

## Part H — Offline, maintenance and support

Only the offline path shown by the app is available for counter sales. Open tabs work offline too: open a tab, add rounds with Hold, and settle — all queue on the device and send in order (tab first, then the sale) when the connection returns, with weeks-old work attributed to the open shift at send time and original dates kept. With two tills on one network, tabs, shifts, menu changes, pack sizes, new products, modifiers, promotions, deliveries, counts, drawer openings and opening/closing checklists also travel directly between the tills over the local mesh, so the second till keeps selling through a long outage; each till also shows who is in shift, drawer cash movements and counts, and submitted cash-ups from the other till. Sign each staff member in on each till before going offline, because sign-ins, PINs, cash drops, counts and cash-up reviews never travel between tills — only their visible results do — and everything reconciles when the connection returns. Uncertified tab totals show as estimates; two terminals changing one tab sends the conflict to manager review instead of guessing. Bar expenses, opening/closing checklists, and void requests queue offline the same way (void approval itself always happens on the server at send time, never on the device).

Selling never pauses for a stock check: if the server check fails, the Till sells on this till's last known stock (saying so on screen) instead of blocking every product, and the server still refuses any genuine over-sale at Pay. Shift close and the shared drawer are offline-first: clock in and out, cash-up submits, drawer opening, drops, paid-outs, handovers, blind close submits and both cash-up reviews queue on the device with the operator or manager PIN already proven, and send in the same order with the same safety when the connection returns (a sale waits behind its shift's cash-up; a clock-out waits behind a provisional cash-up). Reviews done offline are provisional — the screen says they are saved on this device — until the server confirms them against its totals; an approval becomes final at sync. Selling needs an open drawer period on a shared outlet even offline: open the drawer first (opening queues too), or the Till refuses with the reason. If a drawer action answers with a timeout instead of a result, it may already be recorded: refresh the drawer list first and only retry a missing move — the retry replays the original instead of recording twice. Changing how an outlet counts cash (drawer vs pouches) always needs a connection. Device Excel/PDF exports stamp every page UNCONFIRMED and never replace the certified reports. Open-tab changes, setup, complete reports, final cash-up and Accounting tasks require a connection. A product saved offline stays visible in Products as Pending sync until the server confirms it, and the Till already sells it offline at its entered price (marked Pending sync on its card); once the connection returns, new taps wait for the sync while queued sales send in order — product first, then the sale — so nothing is lost or double-charged. A completed offline sale never asks for retry afterwards — only a genuinely interrupted Pay shows the Earlier-sale banner, which collapses to one line and always retries the original attempt, never a new sale. Clocking out requires a submitted cash-up first. Physical Count and Count All queue offline from last-known expected quantities (each line carries its baseline version; the server still rejects a stale baseline to manager review instead of guessing); Receive and Waste queue offline the same way. Certified stock labels still need a reconnect and refresh. Changing the operating profile offline saves on this computer only and warns — save again when back online. If Sell shows **Stock status unavailable — selling is paused**, restore the connection in Manage > System Health and choose **Refresh** in the Sell warning. If Sell shows an earlier verified time instead, it is selling on last verified stock data: refresh when online. The Till checks the server again; wait for product status to return before adding or charging an item. If the warning remains, keep sales paused and give the manager the time and outlet. Pending local work, cloud records, exports and recoverable backups are different things. Reconnect, wait for a final status, check the original record and never create a second payment because a first attempt timed out.

Use Manage > Data & backup for supported exports/backups, Manage > System Health for connectivity, pending work and failed operations (Retry re-queues the original operation; Clear stops its retries and records the item for manager review), the Devices & displays tab for printer, drawer, scanner, screen and card-machine setup with its readiness strip, Test all devices, network printer finder and morning check, the Other tills panel to confirm the second till is linked, the Daily checks tab for the Open the bar and Close the bar routines with the card-totals compare, and Manage > Audit trail for traceable events. A lost customer slip is reprinted with the big Reprint last receipt button on an empty Sell basket. If the power went off, the Till offers the unfinished sale back instead of losing it. A manager locked out of Manage chooses Forgot PIN? Get help on the PIN prompt to send a support request (never type the PIN into the request) and uses Check for reply on the same prompt to read support's answer. Support needs only version, time, outlet, operator role, sanitized error, safe reference and the supported diagnostic bundle. Never send PINs, passwords, card numbers, payroll details or private customer information.

## Printable cards

Opening: confirm business/outlet, connectivity, equipment, float, operator, open tabs, pending work and stock.

Closing: stop or hand over sales, review tabs and uncertain payments, compare expected and counted cash, enter variance reason, submit cash-up and leave a handover note.

Emergency: preserve the original attempt, do not pay twice, open status/recovery, use Check status or Retry original when shown, confirm the record and escalate with a safe reference.
