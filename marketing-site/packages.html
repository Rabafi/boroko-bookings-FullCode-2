LodgingOS

Three packages for accommodation businesses.

LodgingOS supports guest houses, B&Bs, lodges, camps and motels, including lodge and camp operations on nature reserves. Hotels and resorts belong to the separate HotelOS product, which is coming soon. These prices apply only to LodgingOS.

Daily Ops

Starter

P8,999/year

Guest Deposits Lite and the essential front-desk foundation for smaller accommodation businesses that need to run bookings and rooms clearly.

  • Bookings, quotations, invoices, guests, rooms
  • Basic Reports for today, 7-day, and 30-day views with print/PDF
  • Users & Access Lite: up to 2 users (Admin + Receptionist or Operations)
  • Customer-owned backup export with support-led recovery
  • Automatic immutable operational audit evidence
  • Guest Deposits Lite: receive, receipt, ledger, allocate, and Admin-authorized refund/reversal
  • Room board, planning, housekeeping, maintenance
  • Offline support for queued operational actions, with sync status
  • In-app assistant for workflow guidance
  • Up to 120 bookings/month, 6 rooms, 2 users

LodgingOS package fit

Choose the LodgingOS package that fits your accommodation operation.

Choose Starter if

  • You need the core front desk for bookings, guests and rooms
  • You want Guest Deposits Lite and basic operating summaries with print/PDF
  • Your current needs fit Starter's limit of 120 bookings/month, 6 rooms and 2 users

Choose Standard if

  • You need full Staff controls, full Reports and exports, expenses, and Prepayments Management
  • Your current needs fit Standard's limit of 400 bookings/month, 20 rooms and 5 users
  • You need broader management controls for a growing accommodation operation
See the control story behind Standard

Choose Pro if

  • You need direct online reservations and the Manager PWA owner overview
  • You need Credit Control & Automation, POS, inventory, or supplies
  • Your current needs fit Pro's limit of 600 bookings/month, 30 rooms and 10 users

Annual licence terms

  • Starter: P8,999 per year
  • Standard: P12,999 per year
  • Pro: P18,999 per year
LodgingOS upgrade path

What changes when you move between LodgingOS packages.

Starter to Standard

Typically fits when you need full Staff controls, full Reports and exports, expenses, and Prepayments Management.

Standard to Pro

Typically fits when you need Credit Control & Automation, the Manager PWA owner overview, direct online reservations, POS, inventory, and supplies.
