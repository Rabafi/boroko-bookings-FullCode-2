Package fit

How to think about the right package.

Choose Starter if

  • You need to get away from manual booking and room admin first
  • You want a proper front-desk system without paying for the full management layer yet
  • You are willing to upgrade later when owner visibility becomes the next pain point

Choose Standard if

  • You want reports, expenses, staff control, and stronger business management
  • You need the package that most serious lodges should land on
  • You want a package that feels complete without yet going into the full commercial suite
See the control story behind Standard

Choose Pro if

  • You want your own branded reservations page
  • You run a bar or kitchen or need stronger stock and commercial control
  • You want Tsa Bonno to behave like the wider operating system of the property

Why this structure works

  • Starter is useful, but naturally outgrown
  • Standard becomes the obvious serious package
  • Pro stays premium without feeling random or bloated
Upgrade logic

The upgrade path should feel like relief, not pressure.

Starter to Standard

Usually happens when the owner wants reports, money control, staff accountability, and a more serious operational picture.

Standard to Pro

Usually happens when the property wants online reservations, bar or kitchen workflows, inventory, and a more complete commercial setup.

Why this matters

The product story stays easier to explain when each package has a clear emotional and business reason to exist.
