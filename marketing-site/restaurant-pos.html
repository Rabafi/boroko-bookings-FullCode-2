How it works

From first pour to last count. Five steps.

Boroko POS follows the natural flow of a bar shift — open up, take orders, serve, close out, and know exactly where you stand.

1

Open the till

Start your shift with a cash float. The system knows what's in the drawer.

2

Take orders

Tap items on the POS. Orders route to the right station automatically.

3

4

Close tabs
