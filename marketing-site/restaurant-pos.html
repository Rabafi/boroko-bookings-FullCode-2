Floor service, kitchen and menu production
Floor & Service

See every table. Manage every reservation.

A live floor plan shows every table at a glance — colour-coded by status. Staff open tables, assign waiters, transfer tabs, and manage reservations from the same view. No paper waitlist, no sticky notes.

  • ✓ Visual floor grid with colour-coded table status — available, occupied, running, ready
  • ✓ Reservations with 7 statuses — booked, confirmed, waiting, seated, completed, cancelled, no-show
  • ✓ Waitlist entries can include a quoted wait time; staff can send a WhatsApp update when a table is ready
  • ✓ Tab transfers — move a running tab to a different table in one step
  • ✓ Waiter assignment per table with operator or waiter service modes
  • ✓ Group tables by area — main floor, patio, bar counter, VIP

Kitchen & Bar Display

Orders appear instantly. Tickets route by station.

When staff places an order, it appears on the correct station display in real time — cocktails to the bar, burgers to the grill. Every ticket is timed with colour-coded urgency. The analytics view shows prep time averages and bottleneck trends.

  • ● Live ticket board — new, preparing, ready, served columns per station
  • ● Kitchen stations — assign menu items to kitchen, bar, grill, prep stations
  • ● Time-pressure colour coding — emerald, amber, orange, red
  • ● Kitchen timing analytics — average prep time, busiest station, slow ticket identification
  • ● Standalone prep display for dedicated kitchen tablets or TVs

Menu & Production

From menu items to prep batches. All connected.

Build your menu with categories, modifiers, and combos. Link every item to its recipe ingredients so stock deductions and margins are automatic. Record prep batches for sauces, dough, and marinades with actual yield tracking.

  • ✓ Menu items with categories, pricing, and availability toggles
  • ✓ Modifier groups with min/max selections and applies-to-category rules
  • ✓ Combo/meal-deal builder with required and optional item slots
  • ✓ Recipe costing — link each menu item to its ingredient list with waste percentages
  • ✓ Prep batch recording — sauces, dough, marinades with planned vs actual yield
  • ✓ Recipe variance report — theoretical ingredient usage vs actual stock movement

Cash & Close

Open with a float. Close with certainty.

The cash drawer session tracks every cent from opening float to declared close. The daily close checklist catches open tables, pending tickets, and active shifts before the day ends. Managers can generate an owner digest on demand as a one-page performance snapshot.

  • ● Cash drawer sessions — open with float, track transactions, close with counted total, automated variance
  • ● Pre-close checklist — open tables, pending tickets, drawer status, active shifts, unresolved alerts
  • ● Generate an owner digest with today's revenue, orders, low stock, and exceptions
  • ● Settlement reconciliation — compare POS totals to card terminal or mobile money settlements

The Terminal

The transaction screen your staff will actually use.

Speed matters when the bar is three deep. The POS terminal is built for muscle memory — tap a category, pick an item, take payment, done. Touch mode, keyboard shortcuts, and barcode scanning all work out of the box.

Tap-to-Order

Menu grid with categories, modifier options, favourites bar, and search. Add items in two taps. Works with touch screens and mouse.

Payment Methods

18 payment methods including cash, card, bank transfers, Orange Money, MyZaka, Smega, and 9 bank e-wallet variants. Split payments across multiple methods.

Service Modes

Table service with waiter assignment, takeaway, delivery with address capture, and room/event charge. Each mode adjusts the payment and routing flow.

Manager Controls

PIN-based discount authorisation, void approval with reason tracking, tax overrides. Every exception is logged with who, when, and why.

Order and stock workflow

Keep the menu, active orders, stock records and close-of-day steps together in a focused point-of-sale workspace.

Hardware Ready

Receipt printing (ESC/POS with configurable paper width), cash drawer kick, customer-facing display, and kitchen/bar prep displays. Works on tablets, laptops, or dedicated terminals.

How it works

From first pour to last count. Five steps.

Tsa Bonno Restaurant & Bar POS follows the natural flow of a bar shift — open up, take orders, serve, close out, and know exactly where you stand.

1

Open the till

Start your shift with a cash float. The system knows what's in the drawer.

2

Take orders

Tap items on the POS. Orders route to the right station automatically.

3

Serve & track

Kitchen display shows tickets. Staff mark items ready or served. Tabs update in real time.

4

Close tabs

Split bills, apply discounts, accept payment. Cash, card, or mobile — it's all tracked.

5

End of day

Count the drawer. See variance. Review sales, stock, and staff performance. Managers can generate the owner's digest on demand.
