Quick answers

Questions before you get started?

Can we start with Starter and move later?

Yes. The package structure is built to support that path naturally.

How do we send the enquiry?

Click the request button. The form sends your enquiry directly to the Tsa Bonno team through this site, and we usually reply by email within one working day. You can also message us on WhatsApp (+267 72 789 415) or email support@tsabonno.com.

What if we want online reservations too?

That is where Pro becomes most attractive, because it includes the branded reservations page and broader commercial tools.

Can LodgingOS work for a smaller accommodation business?

Yes. Tsa Bonno is especially good for properties that need structure without enterprise complexity.

What do we actually test in the free 1-month trial?

LodgingOS supports guest houses, B&Bs, lodges, camps and motels. You can test bookings, rooms, invoices, and the package that fits your needs, subject to its capacity limits. Hotels and resorts use the separate HotelOS product, which is coming soon.

What if we are not sure which package fits us?

That is normal. The first conversation can help match your accommodation business to a package based on property type, rooms, workflow, and whether you have a bar or kitchen.

Do we need to be very technical to use Tsa Bonno?

No. The aim is to make the system easier to understand for real accommodation teams, not harder.
