Core areas

What the product helps the property do every day.

Bookings and guests

  • Make, edit, and manage bookings with a clearer front-desk workflow
  • Create quotations before a stay is confirmed
  • Convert quotations into bookings as the guest confirms
  • Keep guest information tied to the stay, room, and invoice

Rooms and availability

  • Use the room board for live room decisions and front-desk action
  • Use planning views to see occupancy flow and pressure points ahead
  • Track housekeeping status and maintenance attention around room readiness
  • Keep room-level visibility close to daily operations

Billing and cash control

  • Create invoices from stays and follow balances more clearly
  • Capture payments, deposits, and billing progress
  • Connect the operational side of the stay to the financial side
  • Reduce the drift that happens when billing sits outside the main system

Operational control

  • Track housekeeping work with more clarity than paper-based routines
  • Capture maintenance issues and, when needed, repair costs
  • Manage room supplies with live balances, loading, usage, and stock take
  • See which rooms or stock lines are low before they become a service problem

Business visibility

  • Use reports and expenses to move from daily firefighting to management control
  • Track room contribution more clearly through supply and maintenance cost signals
  • Keep staff roles and business control in the same platform
  • Use an end-of-day check to confirm cash, balances, and anything that still needs management attention

Growth and commercial tools

  • Run sales and inventory for properties with a bar or kitchen
  • Support branded public reservations pages for Pro lodges
  • Give owners stronger oversight beyond the reception desk alone
  • Support the move from simple property operations into a broader hospitality business
Unique to Boroko

Features that competing lodge systems do not have.

Built-in AI assistant

  • Ask questions in plain language — "show me unpaid bookings", "check in Room 5"
  • Understands lodge vocabulary: chalet, rondavel, banda, folio, tab
  • Corrects common misspellings automatically
  • Flags unusual activity — suspicious voids, revenue leakage, payment anomalies
  • Full investigation view with audit trail for flagged transactions

Multi-desk LAN sync

  • Multiple front-desk computers discover each other automatically on the local network
  • Sync bookings, room status, and payments peer-to-peer — no internet required
  • Conflict detection prevents two receptionists booking the same room for overlapping dates
  • Advisory room locks broadcast across all desks so screens show who is editing what
  • Offline sync queues merge intelligently with dependency resolution

Offline-first architecture

  • Every operation works without internet — bookings, payments, POS, inventory
  • Changes queue locally and replay via Supabase RPCs when connectivity returns
  • Financial operations are prioritized in the sync queue
  • Failed items go to a dead-letter queue with auto-retry
  • Cache freshness tracking knows which data may be stale

Manager mobile app (PWA)

  • Install on any phone — no app store needed
  • Real-time dashboard: occupancy, revenue, booking summary
  • Alerts: overdue checkouts, low stock, maintenance, outstanding balances
  • Money sheet: invoices, expenses, night audit, refunds at a glance
  • Staff view: roles, last sign-in, PWA access status

Conference and event bookings

  • Multiple setup types: Theatre, Boardroom, Classroom, U-Shape, Banquet, Cocktail
  • Catering toggle with notes
  • Deposit tracking with payment status
  • Date conflict detection prevents double-booking
  • Visible in the manager mobile app

Pool and day-use management

  • Configurable templates: Pool Access, Facility Chill, Braai/Barbecue
  • Multiple pricing modes: per person, flat fee, hourly, or package
  • Resource allocation with conflict detection
  • Extras linked to inventory — firewood, braai setup, food items auto-deplete
  • Walk-in workflow for day visitors

In-app support and messaging

  • Managers send requests to front desk from the mobile app
  • Front desk escalates issues to management
  • Threaded conversations with sender identification
  • Push notifications on responses
  • Keeps operational conversations out of scattered WhatsApp chats

Role-based access control

  • 80+ granular permissions — who can manage bookings, void sales, issue refunds
  • Predefined roles: owner, manager, receptionist, housekeeper, accountant
  • Custom overrides per staff member
  • POS outlet scoping — restrict staff to specific outlets
  • Staff lifecycle: active, suspended, archived with audit trail
