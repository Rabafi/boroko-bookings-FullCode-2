Core areas

What the product helps the property do every day.

Bookings and guests

  • Make, edit, and manage bookings with a clearer front-desk workflow
  • Create quotations before a stay is confirmed
  • Convert quotations into bookings as the guest confirms
  • Keep guest information tied to the stay, room, and invoice

Rooms and availability

  • Use the room board for live room decisions and front-desk action
  • Use planning views to see occupancy flow and pressure points ahead
  • Track housekeeping status and maintenance attention around room readiness
  • Keep room-level visibility close to daily operations

Billing and cash control

  • Create invoices from stays and follow balances more clearly
  • Capture payments, deposits, and billing progress
  • Connect the operational side of the stay to the financial side
  • Reduce the drift that happens when billing sits outside the main system

Operational control

  • Track housekeeping work with more clarity than paper-based routines
  • Capture maintenance issues and, when needed, repair costs
  • Manage room supplies with live balances, loading, usage, and stock take
  • See which rooms or stock lines are low before they become a service problem

Business visibility

  • Use reports and expenses to move from daily firefighting to management control
  • Track room contribution more clearly through supply and maintenance cost signals
  • Keep staff roles and business control in the same platform
  • Use an end-of-day check to confirm cash, balances, and anything that still needs management attention

Growth and commercial tools

  • Run sales and inventory for properties with a bar or kitchen
  • Support branded public reservations pages for Pro lodges
  • Give owners stronger oversight beyond the reception desk alone
  • Support the move from simple property operations into a broader hospitality business
