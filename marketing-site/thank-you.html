A quick Windows note

A security message is expected on our first releases

Our Windows installer is not yet digitally signed, so Windows may show “Windows protected your PC” or “Unknown publisher.” This does not mean that your download failed.

  1. Make sure you started the download from the official Tsa Bonno website.
  2. Select More info in the Windows message.
  3. Confirm it is the Tsa Bonno installer you just downloaded, then select Run anyway.

If anything looks unfamiliar, stop and ask us to confirm the installer.

Setup guide

From download to your first workday.

1

Run the installer

Open the Tsa Bonno installer you just downloaded. If Windows asks for confirmation, use the checked steps above.

2

Set up your business

Launch the app and follow the setup wizard for your selected product. Add accommodation details and rooms in LodgingOS, or configure your venue and items in Restaurant or Bar POS. Trial access follows the selected product and package, with applicable limits.

3

Team access

Set up team access using the accounts and permission controls included with your selected product and package.

4

Start using your workflows

Use the workflows included with your selected product: accommodation operations, restaurant table service, or bar counter sales. Check the pricing page for package features and limits.
