Setup guide

From download to first booking in minutes.

1

Run the installer

Open the Boroko-Bookings-1.3.15-x64.exe file you just downloaded. Windows may show a security prompt — click Run anyway.

2

Create your lodge

Launch the app and follow the setup wizard. Add your lodge name, rooms, room types, and rates. The free trial includes full access to all features.

3

Staff accounts

Add your reception and operations staff. Each staff member gets their own login. You control permissions and roles.

4

Start operating

Take bookings, check in guests, create invoices, manage bar or kitchen sales, and run your front desk. Everything is connected from day one.
