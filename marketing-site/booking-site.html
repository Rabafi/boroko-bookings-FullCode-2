What guests see

A complete booking experience, branded to your lodge.

Your booking page is designed to feel like part of your lodge — not a generic third-party form. Every element is branded with your name, logo, and colours.

The Guest Journey

1

Find your lodge

Guests arrive via your unique link — booking.yourlodge.com or a link on your website.

2

Search dates

Pick check-in and check-out dates. The system instantly shows available rooms with prices.

3

Browse rooms

Photo carousels, room types, amenities, nightly rates, and estimated stay totals. Sort by price or capacity.

4

Send a request

Fill in name, email, phone, number of guests, and special requests. The lodge confirms within 24 hours.

Page Features

Photo Galleries

Each room has a swipeable photo carousel with a full-screen lightbox. Photos are automatically optimised for fast loading on any device.

WhatsApp Contact

Guests can reach you directly via WhatsApp from the booking page. Show up in their phone with one tap — no email tag needed.

Google Calendar

After booking, guests can add their stay to Google Calendar with one click. Booking reference, room, and dates are pre-filled.

SEO Optimised

Your booking page includes structured data (JSON-LD), Open Graph tags, and dynamic meta descriptions — so guests find you on Google.

Branded Design

Your lodge name, logo, hero image, colours, and tagline. The page feels like yours — not a generic booking form.

Customisable FAQ

Add your own FAQs — cancellation policy, payment terms, check-in times, house rules. 12 default questions included, fully editable.

Why direct bookings matter

Stop paying commission. Let guests find you.

Online travel agencies charge 15-20% commission on every booking. Your own booking page puts that money back in your pocket. Guests book direct, you keep the margin.

No commission fees

Every booking goes directly to you. No middleman taking a cut. A single direct booking per month can pay for the entire Pro subscription.

Your brand, your relationship

Guests see your lodge name and logo — not a third-party brand. You own the guest relationship from first click to checkout.

Instant availability

Rooms and rates are pulled live from your Tsa Bonno system. No manual updates. No double-bookings. What guests see is always current.

How it connects

Bookings go straight into your system.

When a guest sends a booking request, it appears instantly in your Tsa Bonno desktop app. Front desk reviews the dates, confirms or contacts the guest, and the booking is live. No copy-pasting. No missed requests.

1

Guest sends request

Online form captures guest details, room preference, and dates.

2

Appears in Tsa Bonno

The request shows up in your online bookings queue with all guest details.

3

Front desk confirms

Review dates, confirm availability, and contact the guest with payment details.

4

Booking is live

The room is booked, the guest is in your system, and the room board updates automatically.

Built for mobile

Guests book from their phone. It just works.

The booking site is fully responsive with touch-optimised interactions. Photo carousels swipe smoothly, dates pick easily, and the form works perfectly on any screen size.

Touch-optimised

Swipeable photo galleries, large tap targets, and a booking form that works with virtual keyboards. Designed for thumbs, not mice.

Fast loading

Images are served in WebP format with responsive srcSet. Skeleton loading states keep the experience smooth even on slow connections.

Offline aware

If a guest loses connection mid-booking, the form data is saved. An offline banner lets them know, and they can resume when back online.
