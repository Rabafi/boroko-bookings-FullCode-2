What it fixes

Most lodge owners do not need more software noise. They need fewer blind spots.

Money is easier to trust

Outstanding balances, invoices, collections, expenses, and refunds stop living in separate conversations.

Operations are easier to follow

Room status, maintenance, housekeeping, and unresolved issues become visible before they become guest problems.

Management gets faster

Instead of waiting for manual updates, owners can see what changed and where attention is needed right now.

What owners can see

A clearer operational picture across the property.

Bookings and room pressure

See arrivals, departures, in-house guests, occupancy, blocked rooms, and booking issues without asking reception to explain the day.

Balances and cash movement

Track unpaid bookings, invoicing status, end-of-day balancing, expenses, refunds, and the money questions that usually create stress.

Stock and commercial activity

Watch POS activity, inventory pressure, room supplies, and where quiet leakage starts when the business is busy.

Staff accountability

Use role-based access, audit visibility, and activity history to understand what was done, what is still pending, and where follow-through is weak.

Connected ecosystem

The control layer works because the rest of Tsa Bonno is connected.

This is not a separate dashboard pretending to know the business. It sits on top of the same operating system used by reception, management, and direct online bookings.

Desktop App feeds the truth

Front desk activity, room changes, invoices, POS actions, housekeeping, and stock updates all flow from daily operations.

Manager App extends visibility

Owners can keep watching from a phone when they are away from the lodge, without losing the wider management picture.

Booking Site adds direct demand

Online requests come into the same system, so direct reservations become part of the real operating picture instead of a side channel.
