Manager Mobile App

Your business in your pocket. See what matters, from anywhere.

The Tsa Bonno Manager app supports authorised Lodge, Hotel, Restaurant, and Bar owners and managers, with availability scoped by product and package. Guest Deposit balances are read-only on mobile; receiving, allocating, refunding, reversing, reconciling, exporting, matching, and configuration remain in the controlled desktop workflow. No install needed; it works in any browser.

What you can do

Everything a manager needs. Nothing they don't.

The Manager app is designed for owners and managers who need to stay informed without being tied to the front desk.

Dashboard & Overview

KPI Dashboard

Today's arrivals, departures, occupancy percentage, outstanding balances, open alerts, and pending online booking requests — all at a glance.

Needs Attention

A prioritised list of what needs action now — overdue checkouts, pending online requests, urgent maintenance, low stock, and top outstanding balances.

Today Timeline

See arrivals, departures, and activity that happened since you opened the app. Know what changed while you were away.

Bookings & Rooms

Booking List & Calendar

Switch between list and calendar views. Filter by today, in-house, arrivals, departures, online requests, overdue, or upcoming. Search by guest name.

Room Grid

Visual room status at a glance — colour-coded for clean, dirty, cleaning, out-of-service, occupied, and maintenance. Filter by status.

Booking Details

Guest name, dates, status, source, total, paid amount, balance, and payment status — all in a bottom sheet on your phone.

Money & Reports

Shift Snapshot

Gross collected, refunds today, expenses today, and outstanding balance. Know the financial position without asking front desk.

Reports

Read-only visibility into occupancy, server-confirmed money views, expenses, outstanding payments, POS revenue, and more. Pro lodge oversight does not post financial changes.

Night Audit

Run end-of-day audit for any date. See check-ins, check-outs, new bookings, gross collected, refunds, net cash, expenses, and outstanding.

Communication & Alerts

Inbox — Chat with Front Desk

Start conversations or reply to existing threads. Send requests, ask for updates, and get responses — like a private chat between you and your team.

Push Notifications

Get notified about urgent alerts, overdue checkouts, maintenance updates, outstanding balances, and front desk replies — even when the app is closed.

Alerts Board

Overdue checkouts, open maintenance (urgent/high), unpaid bookings, low stock, and online bookings blocked by maintenance — all filterable by category.

How it works

Managers see and ask. Front desk does.

The Manager app is built around a simple principle: managers monitor the business and send approved operational requests. Front desk staff execute controlled financial changes on the desktop app. This keeps financial operations controlled while giving managers the visibility their package allows.

1

Open the app

Works in any mobile browser. No install needed. Add to home screen for app-like experience.

2

See what's happening

Dashboard shows today’s server-backed numbers. Alerts show what needs attention. Reports show trends.

3

Send a request

Tap "Ask front desk to..." on any alert. A request goes straight to the team via the inbox.

4

Get notified

Push notifications tell you when the team responds or when something needs your attention.

Desktop vs Mobile

What managers can and can't do on mobile

The Manager app is read-only for Guest Deposits and other financial changes. Selected operational actions and communication remain available where the product, package, capability, and server contract permit them.

Managers CAN

  • ✓ View all bookings, rooms, guests, and invoices
  • ✓ View finances, expenses, and reports
  • ✓ Review night-audit checks and exceptions
  • ✓ Raise maintenance tickets where authorised
  • ✓ Send approved requests to front desk
  • ✓ Chat with front desk via inbox
  • ✓ View inventory and low-stock alerts
  • ✓ View staff roster and roles

Front desk DOES on desktop

  • ⚙ Create, edit, or cancel bookings
  • ⚙ Process payments and refunds
  • ⚙ Create and send quotations
  • ⚙ Update room housekeeping status
  • ⚙ Manage POS orders and inventory
  • ⚙ Add or adjust expenses
  • ⚙ Enter day-use and conference bookings
  • ⚙ Full data import and export
Works everywhere

No install. No app store. Just open your browser.

The Manager app is a Progressive Web App (PWA). It works in Chrome, Safari, or any modern browser on Android or iPhone. Add it to your home screen and it feels like a native app.

Push Notifications

Get real-time alerts about urgent issues, overdue checkouts, maintenance updates, and front desk replies — even when the browser is closed.

Offline Caching

View cached dashboard data, bookings, rooms, and reports when connectivity is limited. Approved operational changes can queue for retry; cached financial values remain clearly non-authoritative.

Install to Home Screen

Add the app to your phone's home screen for one-tap access. It opens in standalone mode without the browser address bar.

Dark Mode

Toggle dark mode for comfortable viewing at night or in low-light environments. Your preference is saved.
