FAQ

Common questions from prospective lodges.

Is Boroko Bookings only for large properties?

No. It is especially strong for small and growing lodges that need a practical system without enterprise complexity.

Can we start small and upgrade later?

Yes. The package structure is built to let lodges start with essential operations and upgrade when owner control or growth needs increase.

Does Pro include our own online reservations page?

Yes. Pro is the package for lodges that want their own branded public reservations page and stronger control over online bookings.

Can Boroko handle stock and bar or kitchen sales too?

Yes. Pro includes sales, inventory, room supplies, and stronger commercial workflows across the property.

What happens during the free 7-day demo?

You use Boroko with your real lodge setup for 7 days so you can see how bookings, rooms, invoices, and other workflows feel in real life.

Do you help us understand the system during the demo?

Yes. The goal of the demo is not just to hand you software. It is to help you see whether Boroko makes sense for your lodge.

Will my staff struggle to learn it?

Boroko is designed to be easier to understand than many generic systems, especially for reception and day-to-day lodge operations.

What if we only want to fix bookings first?

That is fine. Many lodges can begin with the operational basics first and then grow into stronger management or commercial tools later.
