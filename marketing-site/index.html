FAQ

Common questions from hospitality owners.

Is Tsa Bonno one application with different labels?

No. Lodge & Camp, Hotel, and Restaurant & Bar POS have separate workspaces, installers, operating language, and commercial packages.

Which application should I choose?

Choose Lodge & Camp for accommodation-led properties, Hotel for hotel-native front desk and night audit, Restaurant POS for table and kitchen service, or Bar POS for focused counter sales. PropertyOS, coming soon, serves estate agencies, property managers, and facilities providers.

What is PropertyOS?

PropertyOS is the coming-soon Tsa Bonno application for property businesses, with separately purchasable Agency, Management, and Facilities modules. It is in development; pricing and launch dates are not decided yet.

Does Pro include our own online reservations page?

Yes. Pro is the package for lodges that want their own branded public reservations page and stronger control over online bookings.

Can Tsa Bonno handle stock and bar or kitchen sales too?

Yes. Pro includes sales, inventory, room supplies, and stronger commercial workflows across the property.

What happens during the free 1-month trial?

You use the application that matches your business with a real operating setup for 1 month, so your team can judge the daily workflow before committing.

Do you help us understand the system during the demo?

Yes. The goal is not just to hand you software. It is to confirm which Tsa Bonno product and package fits the way your business operates.

Will my staff struggle to learn it?

Each application uses the language and navigation of the business it serves, so teams do not have to work through unrelated modules.

What if we only want to fix bookings first?

That is fine. Many lodges can begin with the operational basics first and then grow into stronger management or commercial tools later.
