Everything you need

No fluff. Just the tools that sell drinks.

Tsa Bonno Bar POS does one thing: help you sell drinks, track your money, and close without stress.

Quick Sell Terminal

Categories for beers, spirits, cocktails, shots. Tap a drink, set quantity, take payment. Two taps, done. Works with touch screens and mouse.

Payment Methods

Cash, card, Orange Money, MyZaka, SMEGA, bank transfer. Split payments across methods. All recorded with an audit trail.

Stock Tracking

Every sale deducts stock automatically. Know what's low. Know what to order. Set reorder levels and get low-stock warnings.

Staff Clock-In

Staff sign in when they arrive, sign out when they leave. Know who worked when. Track sales per staff member.

Cash Drawer & Close

Open with a float. The system tracks what you should have. Count your drawer. Variance is calculated automatically. Close with confidence.

Offline-First

Works without internet. No WiFi? No problem. Orders, payments, and stock adjustments all save locally. Sync when you're online.

Manager Mobile App

Included for authorised managers and admins. Check live sales, open tabs, stock alerts, cash-close readiness, reports, and team activity from a phone. It is read-only; selling and cash-up stay on the Bar terminal.
