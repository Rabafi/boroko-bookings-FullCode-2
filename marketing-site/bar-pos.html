Everything you need

The tools your bar needs throughout the day.

Tsa Bonno Bar POS does one thing: help you sell drinks, track your money, and close without stress.

Quick Sell Terminal

Categories for beers, spirits, cocktails, shots. Tap a drink, set quantity, take payment. Use a touch screen or mouse to record the sale.

Payment Methods

Cash, card, Orange Money, MyZaka, SMEGA, bank transfer. Split counter sales across methods with an audit trail. Card and mobile money are recorded after collection through your approved provider. Pool-table sales are cash-only: card, mobile money, split and account charge are disabled for a sale holding Pool lines.

Stock Tracking

Every sale deducts pack stock automatically. Set reorder levels and get low-stock warnings in Base. Supplier purchasing, reorder suggestions, lot and expiry tracking, recipe-linked stock, prep and wastage records, valuation and advanced stock margin control are Stock & Purchasing Pro features when confirmed for your commissioned release.

Staff Clock-In

Staff clock in when they arrive and out when they leave, and sales report by operator. Labour cost, payroll and workforce analytics are excluded from Base and need the Accounting & Workforce add-on (P3,500/year) together with Stock & Purchasing Pro. The two add-ons cost P6,000/year combined.

Cash Drawer & Close

Open with a float. The system tracks what you should have. Count your drawer. Variance is calculated automatically. Close with confidence.

Sales and stock workflow

Record drink sales, follow stock movements and review till activity from a focused bar workspace.

Manager Mobile App

Included for authorised managers and admins. When connected, check sales, open tabs, stock alerts, cash-close readiness, reports, and team activity from a phone. It is read-only; selling and cash-up stay on the Bar terminal. Internet and mobile data service are supplied by your provider.

A more confident close

See what the till says, then count what is there.

At the end of a shift, staff submit their cash count. An authorised supervisor can review it against the recorded till total, see any difference and decide what needs attention.

  • Review open tabs before closing.
  • See the expected and counted cash together.
  • Return a count for correction when needed.
