Common switch triggers
  • Too many moving parts spread across books, spreadsheets, and chats

Problems Boroko solves

The practical reasons lodges decide enough is enough.

Manual booking admin takes too much energy

When bookings, room status, and guest notes live in different places, reception becomes slower and mistakes become easier.

Owners still cannot see the business clearly

Even when the lodge is busy, the money story, cost story, and operations story are often still disconnected.

Stock and supplies disappear quietly

Without stronger tracking, room supplies and bar or kitchen stock create waste, frustration, and surprise shortages.

Growth becomes messy

As soon as the lodge adds more staff, a bigger bar or kitchen, or online reservations, weak systems start getting exposed quickly.

What changes after switching

Boroko is designed to make the lodge feel more organised, more visible, and more manageable.

Day-to-day work gets cleaner

Bookings, rooms, quotations, guests, and invoices stop feeling like separate jobs.

Management gets clearer

Reports, expenses, staff control, and operational follow-through start feeding the same picture.

Growth gets more realistic

The lodge can move into online reservations, sales, inventory, and broader commercial control with less chaos.
